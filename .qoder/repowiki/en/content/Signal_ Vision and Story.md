## What SIGNAL is

SIGNAL is the first 48-hour AI-native viability environment: real enterprise AI problems, tackled by AI-native talent on AI-native infrastructure, producing evidence-grade commercial intelligence — not pitch decks. It is co-hosted by Warwick Business School and OneMundi. It is not a hackathon and not a conference; the founding document (`IDEA.md`) is explicit about that.

## The Wow Page promise

The Wow Page is the personal front door to SIGNAL. Instead of one generic landing page, each person receives a page composed specifically for them — reflecting their skills, experience, and goals, grounded in their actual profile, building itself in front of them with visible steps. The core promise every later decision serves: *the platform understands you before asking anything of you.* Two constraints follow directly: no generic filler, no hallucinated achievements — every claim on a page must be grounded in the network graph.

## The demo that anchors the MVP

The MVP is anchored to a concrete event: a meeting with the Dean of Warwick Business School on 2026-08-24. The demo gates on "the page composes live in front of the audience"; the sub-10-second latency target is explicitly post-demo. Four synthetic personas (Miriam, Jonas, David, Tobias in `personas/`) are the acceptance set: the MVP is acceptable when each of them receives a visibly distinct, grounded page.

## What this project is and is not

It is a demonstration that personalized, AI-composed experiences are viable today; a showcase for the SIGNAL brand; a foundation for future matching between talent, enterprises, and outcomes; built with transparency (the user sees how the page was made). It is not a replacement for human judgment, not an unguarded free-form generation playground, not an engineer-only demo, and not a finished product — it is a living prototype that grows with SIGNAL.

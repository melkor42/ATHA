What is the vision and mission of ATHA?
Core formulation
Vision
A world in which AI-native innovation creates extraordinary business and societal value while making work healthier, more joyful, more responsible, and more human.
Mission
ATHA brings education, research, industry, talent, experience design, and AI-native transformation together to solve consequential real-world challenges, develop future changemakers, and demonstrate that outstanding innovation and human flourishing can strengthen one another.
The vision of ATHA
ATHA envisions a new era of innovation in which exceptional outcomes no longer depend on exhaustion, pressure, fragmentation, or the reduction of people to execution capacity.
In this future:
- AI is understood as contextual intelligence, not merely automation.
- People and AI create together without surrendering human judgment or responsibility.
- Organizations consider whether an AI direction is valuable, scalable, responsible, and genuinely desired before implementing it.
- Education develops changemakers capable of navigating the complete human, commercial, ethical, and technological complexity of the AI-native era.
- Industry, research, education, and emerging talent collaborate continuously.
- Healthy environments, rest, nourishment, connection, and joy support ambitious work rather than being treated as distractions from it.
- Innovation produces stronger commercial outcomes while respecting the human beings and wider systems it affects.
ATHA’s vision is not simply to create better events.
It is to influence how organizations innovate, how people are educated, how AI opportunities are assessed, and how human beings experience the work of building the future.
ATHA envisions innovation environments in which people produce their most meaningful and ambitious work because they are supported as complete human beings.
The mission of ATHA
ATHA’s mission is to create and operate AI-native innovation experiences and a global ecosystem through which people and organizations can:
- work on real and consequential industry challenges;
- understand AI through a more holistic perspective;
- make better decisions about whether and how AI should be used;
- experience healthier and more human ways of working;
- develop the capabilities required for the AI-native era;
- connect education, research, talent, and industry;
- create evidence about the relationship between human conditions and innovation outcomes;
- turn promising insights into real organizational transformation.
ATHA fulfills this mission through recurring physical experiences, a continuing digital ecosystem, a living methodology, research partnerships, and long-term relationships among participants and institutions.
What ATHA brings together
ATHA connects participants who are normally separated:
Education
Students, alumni, educators, and institutions develop the capabilities required to become AI-native changemakers.
Research
Research centers and academic partners help study, challenge, measure, and strengthen the methodology and its claims.
Industry
Organizations bring real challenges, commercial context, implementation opportunities, and consequential decisions.
Talent
Participants demonstrate and develop their ability to work through context, responsibility, creativity, collaboration, and AI.
OneMundi
OneMundi builds, connects, operates, and stewards ATHA. It develops the transformation methodology and creates pathways from ATHA insights into industry implementation.
Warwick Business School
Warwick Business School enables ATHA through education, facilities, research, networks, students, alumni, institutional reach, and academic credibility.
Hadora
Hadora designs the lived human experience: the environment, rhythm, nourishment, restoration, movement, connection, and atmosphere through which ATHA works differently.
The mission in practice
ATHA’s mission is expressed through five commitments.
1. Create a different experience
ATHA designs a working environment in which ambitious creation can coexist with:
- rest;
- nourishing food;
- movement;
- meditation and breathwork;
- reflection;
- nature alignment;
- connection;
- joy and belonging.
These conditions are part of the innovation method, not hospitality surrounding it.
2. Produce better AI decisions
ATHA helps industry partners assess:
- whether meaningful value exists;
- whether the direction is scalable;
- whether it is responsible;
- whether it is genuinely desired;
- what human and organizational consequences it creates;
- whether the idea should proceed, stop, wait, or change.
ATHA does not assume every challenge should produce a prototype or implementation.
3. Develop AI-native changemakers
ATHA gives students, researchers, alumni, and industry participants an opportunity to experience a different way of thinking, collaborating, deciding, and building.
It develops people capable of combining:
- human and artificial intelligence;
- contextual understanding;
- commercial judgment;
- ethics and responsibility;
- entrepreneurship and implementation;
- creativity and collaboration;
- stewardship and accountability.
4. Build evidence
ATHA investigates whether healthier and more human environments can produce:
- stronger collaboration;
- more original ideas;
- better decisions;
- more responsible solutions;
- greater participant aliveness;
- more sustainable and long-lasting outcomes.
These relationships must be researched rather than assumed.
5. Build a global ecosystem
ATHA is not intended to end with the experience.
Participants and partners remain connected through a digital platform, future experiences, shared learning, research, talent relationships, new challenges, and transformation opportunities.
The ATHA experience activates the ecosystem. The ecosystem compounds its value.
The ambition beyond the first ATHA
The first ATHA is provisionally planned as a two- to three-day experience in London and Coventry, co-hosted by OneMundi and Warwick Business School.
The larger ambition is to create:
- a recurring international experience series;
- a global network of aligned educational and research institutions;
- a trusted community of industry partners;
- a development environment for future AI-native talent;
- a living, jointly owned methodology;
- a growing body of research and evidence;
- a digital ecosystem preserving relationships and shared context;
- a pathway from innovation insight into organizational transformation.
ATHA aims to help shape a new era of collaboration among business, education, research, talent, and AI.
What success looks like
ATHA succeeds when:
- industry partners leave with decisions and insights they could not have reached alone;
- senior leaders experience a way of working they want to bring into their organizations;
- participants produce outstanding work without unnecessary exhaustion or pressure;
- students and alumni develop into credible AI-native changemakers;
- researchers generate defensible knowledge and new questions;
- partners remain active in the ecosystem;
- challenge insights lead to responsible implementation;
- the methodology becomes stronger after every activation;
- the experience itself is considered worth the commitment;
- the resulting value makes continued collaboration compelling.
Final synthesis
Vision: A new era of innovation in which AI, human intelligence, education, research, and industry create extraordinary value through environments that are healthy, responsible, joyful, and deeply human.
Mission: Build and operate transformative AI-native experiences and a global ecosystem that solve real industry challenges, develop future changemakers, generate research and evidence, and create pathways from insight into responsible organizational transformation.
Ultimate ambition: Make ATHA a global home for people and organizations who want to build the AI-native future differently - with greater intelligence, greater responsibility, and greater humanity.

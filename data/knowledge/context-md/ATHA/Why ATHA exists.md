Why does ATHA exist?
Short answer
ATHA exists to demonstrate that exceptional AI-native innovation can be more ambitious, more responsible, and more commercially valuable while also being healthier, more joyful, and more human.
It brings education, research, industry, talent, experience design, and organizational transformation together around real AI challenges.
ATHA exists because neither conventional business events nor conventional AI initiatives create the complete environment needed for the AI-native era.
ATHA creates a place where people can experience, investigate, and begin building a different future of work, innovation, and human-AI collaboration.
1. Because AI is being understood too narrowly
Most organizations currently approach AI as:
- another technology;
- an automation opportunity;
- a collection of tools and agents;
- a way to reduce cost;
- a way to increase output;
- a faster execution layer.
This approach may create local efficiencies, but it does not unlock AI’s wider organizational potential.
ATHA exists to help organizations understand AI as contextual intelligence.
Instead of asking only, “What can AI do?”, ATHA asks:
- What is the complete context?
- Should this direction be pursued?
- What value could it create?
- Is it scalable?
- Is it responsible?
- Is it genuinely desired?
- What human consequences will it produce?
- How would the organization need to change?
- What should remain under human judgment and responsibility?
ATHA moves the conversation from isolated implementation toward the design of a genuinely AI-native organization.
2. Because “can we build it?” is the wrong first question
Many innovation events reward visible activity and rapid prototypes.
Teams are encouraged to demonstrate something that works, even when the underlying opportunity, desirability, ethics, scalability, or organizational context remain unclear.
ATHA begins earlier.
Before asking whether something can be built, it asks whether it should be built.
The most valuable result may not be a prototype. It may be:
- a new perspective;
- a better question;
- a decision not to proceed;
- the discovery of a hidden risk;
- a shift in direction;
- an entirely different opportunity;
- a deeper understanding of what the organization actually needs.
ATHA exists to improve the quality of the decision before organizations commit resources to implementation.
3. Because outstanding work should not require exhaustion
Many business and innovation formats are built around intensity, competition, time pressure, poor food, insufficient rest, and visible performance.
People may produce something quickly, but the process can reduce reflection, connection, creativity, responsibility, and long-term thinking.
ATHA challenges the assumption that pressure and exhaustion are necessary conditions for exceptional outcomes.
It creates an environment that may include:
- meditation and contemplation;
- breathwork;
- movement and sport;
- walking meditation;
- sound-based restorative experiences;
- meaningful breaks;
- rest and reflection;
- nourishing food;
- informal conversation;
- feedback and connection;
- joy and play;
- healthier rhythms of focused creation.
These are not wellbeing activities added around the real work. They are part of the innovation design.
ATHA exists to explore whether healthier human conditions can produce:
- more original ideas;
- stronger collaboration;
- better decisions;
- more responsible solutions;
- greater creativity;
- deeper contextual understanding;
- more sustainable outcomes;
- an experience people actually want to repeat.
4. Because OneMundi’s philosophy must be experienced
OneMundi’s difference is difficult to communicate through words alone.
Ideas such as contextual intelligence, stewardship, aliveness, human-centered performance, conscious decision-making, and nature-aligned work can sound abstract until people experience them.
ATHA makes the OneMundi thesis tangible.
Senior leaders can observe and participate in:
- meetings conducted differently;
- teams collaborating with less ego;
- AI being used as a contextual counterpart;
- ambition without unnecessary pressure;
- rest without loss of performance;
- responsibility without excessive control;
- connection without sacrificing commercial seriousness;
- people creating from a healthier human state.
ATHA exists so a partner can ask:
What would become possible if our own organization worked more like this?
The experience itself becomes evidence that another organizational reality is possible.
5. Because education and industry are too disconnected
Educational institutions develop knowledge and talent. Industry owns real challenges, context, implementation opportunities, and commercial consequences.
These systems often interact too weakly or too late.
Students may learn theories without experiencing the complete reality of organizational transformation. Companies may adopt AI without access to emerging talent, academic challenge, or a sufficiently broad perspective.
ATHA connects:
- students and alumni;
- faculty and researchers;
- Warwick Business School and other educational institutions;
- industry partners;
- senior decision-makers;
- OneMundi;
- Hadora;
- future technology and implementation partners.
ATHA gives students and researchers access to consequential real-world challenges. It gives industry partners access to new talent, perspectives, research, and ways of working.
6. Because the AI-native era needs different changemakers
The future will not be built only by people who understand AI technology.
It requires people who can combine:
- artificial and human intelligence;
- commercial judgment;
- contextual understanding;
- ethics and responsibility;
- creativity and entrepreneurship;
- collaboration and stewardship;
- emotional and relational awareness;
- organizational design;
- implementation capability.
Warwick Business School’s role in ATHA is not merely to provide participants or facilities. Its deeper interest is to understand how people should be educated to become changemakers in the AI-native era.
ATHA exists as an environment in which future talent can learn by participating in a different reality rather than only studying it.
7. Because OneMundi needs a living methodology
OneMundi begins with founder experience, prior value creation, embodied operating practices, and a basic holistic AI framework.
It does not yet possess a finished, validated methodology.
ATHA provides the environment in which the methodology can be:
- applied to real challenges;
- tested by diverse teams;
- challenged academically;
- observed in practice;
- measured;
- modified;
- versioned;
- improved continuously.
Each ATHA activation should create learning that strengthens the next one.
OneMundi and Warwick Business School intend to own the methodology jointly:
- Warwick develops its educational and research application.
- OneMundi develops its industrial and transformation application.
ATHA exists to turn tacit founder knowledge and early ideas into a rigorous living methodology.
8. Because the OneMundi thesis requires evidence
OneMundi believes human flourishing and exceptional organizational performance can reinforce each other.
That belief should not remain an attractive claim.
ATHA creates an opportunity to investigate questions such as:
- Does rest improve decision quality?
- Do healthier environments improve collaboration?
- Does belonging increase creative contribution?
- Do teams generate more original ideas?
- Does reduced pressure improve contextual understanding?
- Are the resulting AI directions more responsible?
- Do partners implement the insights?
- Do the results retain value over time?
A two- or three-day experience cannot independently prove every long-term claim. ATHA therefore exists as the beginning of a research and evidence program, not as instant validation.
It can generate observations, hypotheses, measurements, and longitudinal relationships through which the larger thesis can be studied responsibly.
9. Because innovation needs an ecosystem, not another event
ATHA is not intended to disappear after the final presentation.
The first experience activates a continuing ecosystem.
Participants and partners should remain connected through:
- a digital platform;
- future ATHA experiences;
- ongoing communication;
- new challenges;
- research;
- talent relationships;
- industry opportunities;
- first value unlocks;
- longer OneMundi transformations.
The event creates the initial energy and relationships. The ecosystem preserves and compounds them.
The ATHA event is the activation point. The ATHA ecosystem is the enduring asset.
ATHA exists to create a long-term international network capable of shaping a new era of innovation, education, business, and AI-native collaboration.
10. Because organizations need a safer entry into transformation
Becoming AI-native is a deep, multi-year transformation.
Many organizations are not ready to commit immediately to changing their structures, culture, leadership, workflows, technology, and governance.
ATHA provides an experiential first step.
A partner can:
- bring a meaningful challenge;
- experience the OneMundi approach;
- receive a holistic assessment;
- understand AI differently;
- meet talent and ecosystem partners;
- identify a credible direction;
- decide whether deeper transformation makes sense.
This lowers the barrier to beginning without reducing the ambition of where the relationship may lead.
Where the opportunity is real, ATHA can open a pathway into a first value unlock and a longer OneMundi transformation partnership.
11. Because business innovation should be joyful
ATHA also exists from a simple human conviction:
Building the future should feel alive.
Innovation does not need to be sterile, exhausting, transactional, or performative.
People can produce serious work while:
- eating well;
- resting;
- laughing;
- moving;
- connecting;
- reflecting;
- spending time in silence;
- enjoying one another;
- feeling that they belong.
Joy does not make the work less ambitious. It can become part of the energy from which ambitious work emerges.
What ATHA ultimately wants to prove
ATHA wants to demonstrate that:
- healthier environments can produce outstanding work;
- AI innovation can begin with responsibility and context;
- human connection can improve rather than obstruct performance;
- education and industry can learn from one another continuously;
- future talent can be developed through lived experience;
- organizations can access AI through a more holistic intention;
- business innovation can be commercially serious and deeply human;
- an event can become the activation point for a global, living ecosystem.
Final synthesis
ATHA exists because the AI-native era requires more than new technology.
It requires:
- a different understanding of intelligence;
- a different way of educating people;
- a different relationship between industry and research;
- a different approach to organizational challenges;
- a different environment for human creation;
- a different standard of responsibility;
- a different experience of ambition and performance.
ATHA brings these elements together.
ATHA exists to create and investigate a new way of building the future - one in which people, organizations, education, and AI produce extraordinary value without sacrificing humanity.

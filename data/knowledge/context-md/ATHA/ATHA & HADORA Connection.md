The Connection Between ATHA and HADORA
HADORA is the philosophy of human experience behind ATHA. ATHA is one specific expression of that philosophy in the world of work, business innovation and artificial intelligence.
Their relationship can be understood simply:
HADORA shapes how the experience happens. ATHA defines what the experience is here to accomplish.
HADORA provides the human foundation
HADORA explores a central question:
How can we create the conditions for people to become more fully human together?
It contributes the principles through which ATHA is designed:
- Human flourishing alongside ambition
- Presence and connection alongside productivity
- Contribution instead of passive participation
- Rhythm, nourishment and recovery as part of the work
- Beauty and care within the environment
- Stewardship rather than excessive control
- Reflection and integration alongside creation
- Temporary culture as a catalyst for lasting change
These principles influence the architecture of ATHA: how people arrive, form relationships, work, eat, rest, reflect, celebrate and return to their wider lives.
ATHA gives HADORA a specific field of application
ATHA applies this philosophy to a defined challenge:
How should humans work, collaborate and innovate in the age of AI?
It brings founders, students, researchers and creatives together around real enterprise challenges. Teams use AI-native methods to develop commercially relevant, technically credible and responsible pathways forward.
ATHA therefore translates the broader HADORA philosophy into a professional and innovation environment.
HADORA asks how people can flourish together.
ATHA asks what this means when people are doing ambitious, consequential work with artificial intelligence.
The connection is experiential, not primarily promotional
HADORA should be deeply present in ATHA without becoming the dominant public-facing brand.
People should first encounter ATHA as a distinctive and credible AI-native innovation experience. They should feel the HADORA influence through the quality of the environment, the rhythm of the work, the depth of connection and the care embedded in every detail.
Only when someone wants to understand why ATHA feels different should they discover the deeper HADORA philosophy behind it.
The appropriate relationship is therefore:
Initiated and owned by OneMundi, developed in partnership and co-hosted with Warwick Business School, and shaped as a HADORA experience.
ATHA should not be positioned as a retreat, a wellbeing programme or simply “an event by HADORA.” It must stand as its own experience and brand.
The exchange works in both directions
HADORA shapes ATHA, but ATHA also helps HADORA evolve.
ATHA becomes a living laboratory in which HADORA’s principles can be tested within demanding, ambitious and commercially relevant work. It provides evidence about whether a human-centred culture can generate stronger decisions, deeper collaboration and exceptional outcomes without producing exhaustion.
The learning from each ATHA experience can then refine both:
- The ATHA methodology for AI-native innovation
- The HADORA approach to designing transformative human experiences
The essential distinction
OneMundi owns the purpose, proposition and delivery of ATHA.
Warwick Business School provides the academic partnership, credibility, community and physical setting.
HADORA shapes the human conditions and experiential philosophy through which ATHA comes alive.
In its simplest form:
ATHA is where the future of AI-native work is explored. HADORA ensures that this future remains deeply human.

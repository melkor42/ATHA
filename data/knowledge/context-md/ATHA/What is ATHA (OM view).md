What is ATHA?
Short answer
ATHA - spelled A-T-H-A and pronounced “Ata” - is a new AI-native business-innovation experience and ecosystem being created by OneMundi in partnership with Warwick Business School and Hadora.
It brings students, researchers, alumni, industry partners, educational institutions, and transformation practitioners together to work on consequential real-world AI challenges.
ATHA combines two ambitions:
	1	Produce outstanding, unexpected, and responsible AI insights for organizations.
	2	Demonstrate that exceptional innovation can happen in an environment that is healthy, balanced, joyful, nourishing, and deeply human.
ATHA is not intended to remain an event.
The ATHA experience is the activation point. The ATHA ecosystem is the enduring asset.
The honest reality today
ATHA is currently an emerging concept and format.
The first experience is provisionally planned for March 2027 in London and Coventry, using Warwick Business School facilities. It is expected to last two to three days and take place across multiple locations.
The complete format, research design, methodology, commercial model, digital platform, partner agreements, and intellectual-property structure still need to be developed.
ATHA should therefore be presented as an ambitious new initiative under development, not as an established global program with a finished methodology.
The full meaning or expansion of the name ATHA has not yet been documented.
Why ATHA exists
Most business-innovation events are organized around speed, pressure, competition, presentations, prototypes, and visible output.
AI events often begin with questions such as:
- What can we automate?
- What prototype can we build?
- Which model or agent should we use?
- How quickly can we demonstrate a result?
- How much cost or effort can we remove?
ATHA begins with a wider question:
What becomes possible when people work AI-native on a real challenge inside an environment deliberately designed for context, health, connection, creativity, responsibility, and human aliveness?
ATHA seeks to demonstrate that people do not need to be exhausted, pressured, or disconnected to produce outstanding work.
It explores whether healthier conditions can create:
- better ideas;
- more original insights;
- stronger collaboration;
- more responsible AI directions;
- more sustainable solutions;
- greater creativity and contextual understanding;
- results with greater long-term relevance;
- an experience people find joyful and nourishing.
The challenge format
Industry partners bring real-world AI challenges into ATHA.
Students, alumni, researchers, and other participants work in multidisciplinary teams to understand the challenge and identify a meaningful direction.
The purpose is not automatically to build a prototype.
Before asking whether something can be built, ATHA asks whether it should be built.
The emerging holistic framework examines questions such as:
- Is there meaningful value in this opportunity?
- Is the proposed direction scalable?
- Is it responsible?
- Is it genuinely desired by the people or market affected?
- What human and organizational consequences could it create?
- Does the organization possess the context and readiness to pursue it?
- Should the idea proceed, stop, wait, or change direction?
A successful result may be:
- a new AI direction;
- a materially improved version of the original idea;
- a decision to proceed;
- a decision not to proceed;
- a decision to delay;
- a discovery that the real opportunity lies somewhere else.
A well-supported “no” or “not yet” can be as valuable as a prototype if it prevents wasted investment or an irresponsible implementation.
The ATHA experience
The physical environment, daily rhythm, food, relationships, and restorative practices are part of ATHA’s innovation method.
They are not hospitality added around the real work.
The experience may include:
- meditation;
- breathwork;
- sound-based restorative practices;
- walking meditation;
- yoga, sport, and movement;
- intentional breaks;
- rest and reflection;
- structured feedback;
- nourishing and balanced food;
- informal conversations;
- time and space for human connection;
- working rhythms designed around energy and attention;
- moments of joy, play, and belonging.
These conditions express the Hadora spirit: ambitious creation inside an environment that supports the complete human being.
Spiritual, contemplative, and somatic practices should remain voluntary. Participants must have accessible alternatives and should never lose belonging, opportunity, or status by declining a particular practice.
The governing boundary is:
Transformative experience without coercion.
The three principal partners
OneMundi
OneMundi carries the primary responsibility for making ATHA real.
Its role includes:
- building and operating the initiative;
- connecting the necessary stakeholders;
- attracting industry partners and challenges;
- creating traction and participation;
- activating the ecosystem;
- developing the holistic AI methodology;
- stewarding the teams and challenge process;
- connecting ATHA insights to industry implementation;
- creating pathways into longer AI-native transformation.
OneMundi is the principal builder, connector, operator, and ecosystem steward.
Warwick Business School
Warwick Business School is the institutional and educational enabler.
Its contribution includes:
- facilities and locations;
- access to students and alumni;
- faculty, research teams, and centers;
- academic and business-school context;
- institutional network;
- educational capability;
- credibility and prestige.
Warwick’s deeper interest is in educating the people capable of becoming changemakers in the AI-native era and developing outstanding talent for the coming decades.
Hadora
Hadora is the experience-design institute within the emerging ATHA model.
Its role is to design the lived human environment:
- place and atmosphere;
- rhythm and restoration;
- nourishment;
- contemplative and somatic practices;
- nature alignment;
- emotional and relational quality;
- the overall experience through which ATHA feels fundamentally different.
OneMundi builds and operates ATHA. Warwick enables it institutionally and educationally. Hadora creates the experiential conditions.

What an industry partner receives
An ATHA industry partner should receive five forms of value.
1. Experience
The partner experiences a different way in which an organization can work, collaborate, rest, use AI, and produce ambitious outcomes.
The experience should make the senior partner ask:
What would become possible if our own organization worked more like this?
2. Perspective
The partner develops a wider understanding of AI.
It moves beyond isolated automation and begins seeing:
- AI as contextual intelligence;
- the relationship between human and artificial intelligence;
- organizational readiness;
- human and cultural consequences;
- responsibility and desirability;
- the importance of environment and working conditions.
3. Decision
The partner receives concrete insight into whether and how it should pursue the challenge.
The result should identify:
- the opportunity;
- the potential value;
- critical assumptions;
- risks and responsibilities;
- scalability;
- human and organizational implications;
- the recommended direction;
- possible next steps.
4. Belonging
The partner enters a community connecting education, research, industry, students, experience design, OneMundi, and Hadora.
The sense of connection and belonging should itself be a meaningful part of the value.
5. Continuity
The relationship does not end after the first experience.
The partner remains part of the ATHA ecosystem and receives:
- a login to the future digital platform;
- continuing communication;
- access to future ATHA experiences;
- ongoing ecosystem relationships;
- the possibility of further collaboration;
- a pathway into OneMundi implementation and transformation.
The current intention is for continuing ecosystem access to be included in the original industry-partner fee. Practical limits, participation places, transferability, capacity, and active-member expectations still need to be defined.
The CEO value test
ATHA should create two “wow” effects.
The first comes from the experience itself:
- being present;
- meeting differently;
- connecting;
- belonging;
- eating and resting well;
- moving and practicing together;
- having meaningful conversations;
- experiencing joy and creative energy;
- seeing ambitious work happen without unnecessary pressure.
The second comes from the challenge outcome:
- original insights;
- a clearer decision;
- an unexpected opportunity;
- a responsible redirection;
- a credible path forward.
ATHA succeeds commercially when the partner believes the experience itself justified participation and the challenge outcome made the value undeniable.
This requires meaningful participation from the relevant CEO, executive sponsor, or senior decision-maker. A partner that submits a challenge and waits remotely for a report will not receive the complete ATHA value.
Rights and intellectual property
The industry partner is intended to own its challenge-specific results, concepts, prototypes, and newly created challenge-specific intellectual property.
That ownership should not transfer the underlying ATHA, OneMundi, Warwick, or Hadora methodology.
A fair provisional structure is:
- the partner owns challenge-specific outputs;
- participants retain pre-existing intellectual property;
- OneMundi and Warwick retain the underlying methodology;
- Hadora retains its general experience-design methods;
- Warwick and researchers retain pre-existing academic methods;
- general learning may be reused only under agreed confidentiality, consent, publication, and anonymization rules;
- third-party technologies remain subject to their existing licenses.
These boundaries must be agreed before confidential partner information is shared or challenge work begins.
What happens after ATHA
Where ATHA identifies a valuable direction, OneMundi may continue supporting the partner.
The follow-on relationship may include:
- refining or redirecting the opportunity;
- creating the first value unlock;
- developing and implementing the solution;
- designing the organizational environment around it;
- establishing decision and governance systems;
- addressing human and cultural implications;
- coordinating additional AI initiatives;
- becoming the organization’s longer-term AI-native transformation partner.
This is a separate paid OneMundi relationship. It is not an unlimited implementation obligation included in the ATHA participation fee.
ATHA as an ecosystem
ATHA should become more than a recurring calendar of experiences.
Its long-term ambition is to build an international ecosystem of like-minded institutions and organizations that work together to shape a new era of innovation, business, education, and AI-native collaboration.
Each ATHA activation can:
- bring new organizations and challenges into the network;
- expose students and leaders to a different operating model;
- create research and evidence;
- identify talent and future stewards;
- originate first value unlocks;
- initiate longer organizational transformations;
- strengthen relationships between education and industry;
- improve the methodology;
- add context and knowledge to the digital ecosystem.
The ecosystem compounds through repeated collaboration, accumulated evidence, shared context, and the movement of people and ideas between education, research, experience, and industry.
ATHA’s methodology
ATHA begins with a seed methodology derived from:
- OneMundi’s founder experience;
- prior rapid value creation;
- entrepreneurial problem-solving;
- holistic AI assessment;
- contextual-intelligence principles;
- human-centered organization design;
- healthier and nature-aligned working;
- Hadora’s experience design;
- Warwick’s educational and research capabilities.
The methodology is not finished.
ATHA is the environment in which it will be tested, challenged, versioned, and continuously improved.
OneMundi and Warwick Business School intend to own the methodology jointly.
Their principal applications are complementary:
- Warwick applies it to education, research, and developing AI-native changemakers.
- OneMundi applies it to industry, organizational implementation, and transformation.
Learning from industry should return to education. New talent and research should return to industry.
The research ambition
ATHA seeks to investigate whether healthier and more human working conditions produce stronger innovation outcomes.
Possible research questions include:
- Do participants create more original ideas?
- Does collaboration improve?
- Does rest improve decision quality?
- Do nourishing environments reduce stress without reducing ambition?
- Are the resulting solutions more responsible or sustainable?
- Do participants experience greater connection and aliveness?
- Are the insights adopted by industry partners?
- Do the results remain valuable over time?
A two- or three-day experience can generate observations and hypotheses. It cannot independently prove long-term impact.
Serious claims require:
- baselines;
- defined measures;
- consent and privacy;
- independent evaluation;
- comparison where feasible;
- longitudinal follow-up;
- separation between evidence and promotional claims.
What ATHA is not
ATHA is not intended to be:
- another business conference;
- a conventional hackathon;
- a student competition;
- a prototype factory;
- an AI sales event;
- an innovation workshop organized around exhaustion;
- a wellbeing retreat disconnected from commercial value;
- a disguised OneMundi sales funnel;
- a one-time event that disappears after the closing presentation.
It is an experience, innovation method, talent environment, research opportunity, industry-decision process, and ecosystem activation.
Comprehensive definition
ATHA is an emerging AI-native business-innovation experience and global ecosystem created by OneMundi with Warwick Business School and Hadora.
It brings education, research, industry, talent, contextual intelligence, and human-centered experience design together around real organizational challenges.
ATHA is designed to produce outstanding and responsible AI insights while demonstrating that ambitious work can be joyful, nourishing, healthy, and deeply human.
Its event format activates the relationships. Its digital platform and future experiences sustain them. Its research develops evidence. Its methodology connects education with industry. Its challenge outcomes create pathways into real implementation and long-term transformation.
ATHA is where people experience, investigate, and begin building a more human AI-native future together.

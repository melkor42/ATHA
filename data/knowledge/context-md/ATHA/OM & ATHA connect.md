What is the connection between ATHA and OneMundi?
Short answer
OneMundi is the company and long-term AI-native transformation partner.
ATHA is an experience, innovation format, public laboratory, talent environment, and global ecosystem created and principally operated by OneMundi with Warwick Business School and Hadora.
The distinction is:
ATHA allows people and organizations to experience, investigate, and begin AI-native transformation. OneMundi helps them implement, operate, and sustain that transformation in the real world.
ATHA is one of the first major expressions of the OneMundi philosophy, but it is not the whole of OneMundi.
The respective roles
OneMundi
OneMundi works with organizations over the longer term to help them become genuinely AI-native.
Its responsibility can include:
- understanding the organization and its people;
- diagnosing AI opportunities;
- designing organizational structures and workflows;
- creating human-AI decision systems;
- implementing contextual-intelligence environments;
- supporting cultural and leadership transformation;
- operating systems and environments where needed;
- measuring human and commercial outcomes;
- remaining a multi-year transformation partner.
ATHA
ATHA creates a concentrated experience in which organizations, participants, researchers, and institutions can explore and demonstrate a different way of working with AI.
Its role includes:
- bringing real industry challenges into a new environment;
- exposing participants to AI-native working;
- assessing opportunities through a holistic framework;
- producing new perspectives and decisions;
- developing future changemakers;
- generating research questions and evidence;
- connecting education, industry, research, talent, and experience;
- activating an ecosystem that continues after the physical experience.
OneMundi is the larger transformation company. ATHA is one of its principal environments for experience, innovation, research, talent, and ecosystem creation.
OneMundi builds and operates ATHA
OneMundi carries the main responsibility for making ATHA real.
It is responsible for:
- developing the concept;
- activating the necessary stakeholders;
- connecting Warwick Business School, Hadora, research centers, industry partners, students, alumni, and other participants;
- attracting challenges and partners;
- creating traction and participation;
- developing the holistic AI-assessment framework;
- operating and stewarding the experience;
- synthesizing challenge outcomes;
- sustaining the ecosystem;
- creating pathways into implementation and transformation.
Warwick Business School is the institutional and educational enabler. It contributes facilities, academic context, students, alumni, researchers, centers, networks, credibility, and educational capability.
Hadora is the experience-design institute. It shapes the human environment, including place, rhythm, nourishment, movement, rest, reflection, contemplative practices, connection, and atmosphere.
The responsibility model is:
Participant|Principal role|
OneMundi|Builder, operator, connector, methodology developer, and ecosystem steward|
Warwick Business School|Educational and institutional enabler|
Hadora|Designer of the lived human experience|
ATHA makes the OneMundi philosophy experienceable
OneMundi’s philosophy can be difficult to communicate through words alone.
Concepts such as:
- AI as contextual intelligence;
- human flourishing as an operating advantage;
- stewardship rather than control;
- healthy ambition;
- presence before reaction;
- collaboration before ego;
- empowerment with accountability;
- better outcomes with less unnecessary pressure;
can sound abstract until people experience them.
ATHA makes these ideas tangible.
Participants and industry partners can experience:
- teams working differently;
- AI being used beyond isolated automation;
- meetings being stewarded with greater context;
- ambitious work occurring alongside rest and nourishment;
- people connecting and creating without unnecessary pressure;
- human and commercial questions being considered together.
ATHA therefore acts as an experiential demonstration of the OneMundi thesis.
OneMundi articulates and implements the transformation. ATHA allows people to feel and observe it.
ATHA is OneMundi’s public laboratory
OneMundi begins with founder experience, previous value creation, an emerging holistic AI framework, and an embodied way of working.
It does not yet possess a finished, independently validated methodology.
ATHA provides an environment in which OneMundi can test and develop its approach through:
- real industry challenges;
- diverse participant teams;
- academic challenge;
- research and observation;
- partner feedback;
- practical implementation questions;
- repeated experiences;
- human and commercial measurement.
Each ATHA activation should improve the OneMundi methodology.
This creates a continuous learning loop:
	1	OneMundi brings its seed methodology into ATHA.
	2	ATHA applies it to real challenges.
	3	Warwick, researchers, participants, and partners test and challenge it.
	4	OneMundi brings the improved methodology into industry.
	5	Industry implementation produces new evidence and questions.
	6	Learning returns to ATHA and Warwick.
	7	The next generation of talent and methodology becomes stronger.
ATHA develops talent for OneMundi and the wider market
OneMundi will need people who can work differently in the AI-native era.
Technical AI capability alone is insufficient. OneMundi needs people capable of combining:
- contextual intelligence;
- commercial judgment;
- human awareness;
- ethics and responsibility;
- creativity and entrepreneurship;
- implementation;
- collaboration;
- stewardship;
- accountability.
ATHA allows students, alumni, researchers, and industry participants to develop and demonstrate these capabilities.
This makes ATHA a potential talent environment for:
- future OneMundi team members;
- AI-native changemakers inside partner organizations;
- researchers and educators;
- ecosystem stewards;
- founders and implementation partners.
Warwick’s educational mission and OneMundi’s industrial mission meet through this talent system.
The jointly owned methodology
OneMundi and Warwick Business School intend to jointly own the methodology developed through ATHA.
Their primary applications are different but complementary.
Warwick’s application
Warwick uses the methodology to:
- educate future AI-native changemakers;
- develop relevant programs and learning experiences;
- conduct research;
- strengthen the connection between education and emerging organizational reality.
OneMundi’s application
OneMundi uses the methodology to:
- assess real industry opportunities;
- design first value unlocks;
- support AI initiatives;
- redesign organizations;
- implement AI-native environments;
- lead longer transformation partnerships.
The methodology connects education with real industry application.
Clear agreements will still be required around ownership, modification, licensing, publication, commercial use, confidentiality, and future development.
ATHA is an entry point into OneMundi transformation
ATHA gives an industry partner a lower-risk and highly experiential way to begin.
The journey can work as follows:
1. Enter ATHA
The partner brings a consequential AI challenge and participates in the experience.
2. Experience the OneMundi model
Senior leaders observe a different relationship between ambition, context, AI, human connection, rest, responsibility, and performance.
3. Receive a challenge direction
The partner receives concrete insight into whether the opportunity is valuable, scalable, responsible, desirable, and worth pursuing.
4. Remain in the ecosystem
The partner receives digital-platform access, continuing relationships, and access to future ATHA experiences.
5. Begin a first value unlock
Where the opportunity is strong, OneMundi can help create a focused and measurable initial result.
6. Enter longer transformation
OneMundi may become the partner responsible for implementing and evolving the organization’s wider AI-native transformation.
ATHA creates the experience, insight, relationship, and opening. OneMundi carries the transformation into the organization.
Commercial connection
An industry partner pays ATHA for:
- the designed experience;
- the challenge assessment;
- access to talent and research;
- challenge-specific outputs;
- ecosystem membership;
- future ATHA access;
- continuing relationships and opportunities.
The partner owns its challenge-specific results, concepts, prototypes, and newly created challenge-specific intellectual property.
A follow-on OneMundi relationship is separate.
The ATHA fee does not include unlimited implementation or organizational transformation. OneMundi’s subsequent work may include:
- refining the opportunity;
- creating the first value unlock;
- implementation;
- organization design;
- contextual-intelligence infrastructure;
- governance;
- wider AI-native transformation.
ATHA must not become a disguised sales event
Although ATHA can originate valuable OneMundi relationships, its purpose cannot be reduced to lead generation.
ATHA must create genuine standalone value for:
- industry partners;
- students;
- researchers;
- Warwick Business School;
- Hadora;
- ecosystem participants.
Partners must remain free to use their outputs without hiring OneMundi afterward.
Research must remain credible. Student participation must not become unpaid sales work. The experience must not pressure organizations into follow-on relationships.
The correct principle is:
ATHA creates value and belonging first. A OneMundi transformation relationship follows only where genuine fit, trust, and opportunity exist.

Brand relationship
The exact brand architecture still needs to be finalized.
A provisional expression could be:
ATHA - initiated and operated by OneMundi, enabled by Warwick Business School, with experience design by Hadora.
ATHA should develop a recognizable identity and ecosystem of its own while remaining clearly connected to OneMundi as its principal builder and operator.
Final synthesis
OneMundi and ATHA have different but interdependent roles.
OneMundi is the transformation company. ATHA is the experience and ecosystem through which that transformation becomes visible, testable, learnable, and accessible.
ATHA allows people to experience a different AI-native reality.
OneMundi brings that reality into organizations.
ATHA develops insights, evidence, relationships, methodology, and talent.
OneMundi turns them into implementation and long-term transformation.
ATHA activates the ecosystem.
OneMundi stewards its connection to industry and organizational change.
Together, they create a bridge from experience to evidence, from education to industry, and from AI ideas to a more human AI-native future.
